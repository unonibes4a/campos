La Gravedad y el Magnetismo

There is a profound correspondence between gravity and magnetism, as both phenomena deform their respective fields in an analogous way. While Albert Einstein taught us that gravity is nothing more than the deformation of the fabric of spacetime, classical physics could traditionally only illustrate this concept through a two-dimensional, flat representation. In this research, Giovanni demonstrates how this deformation truly manifests itself in its actual three-dimensional structure. The field is vectorized by the presence of mass. The vector is a mathematical element used in AI.
